Interactions:
- "Read the newsletter" and "Reach out" fire PostHog events
  (`newsletter_cta`, `reach_out_cta`) via `data-track` attributes.
- Contributions: rows link to the PR; viewport shows exactly 3 rows,
  slides vertically every 4s with blur+fade crossfade; dots jump to a page.
- Marquee pauses on hover; `prefers-reduced-motion` shows a static quote.
