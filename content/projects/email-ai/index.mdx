Email AI is a privacy-first, local-first AI layer for your email inbox. It brings intelligent features to your email workflow without compromising on privacy — all processing happens locally on your machine.

## Features

- Privacy-first architecture with local processing
- AI-powered email intelligence
- Local-first data handling
- Seamless inbox integration

## Tech Stack

Built with TypeScript and Rust, Email AI leverages the performance of Rust for local processing while providing a rich TypeScript-based interface.
